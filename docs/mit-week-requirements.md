Verified 3 November 2026 against [Juknis Web Development MIT-WEEK 2026.pdf](../Juknis%20Web%20Development%20MIT-WEEK%202026.pdf), all eight physical PDF pages (cover is page 1). These references use PDF page numbers, since the document has no printed page numbers. This supersedes the guide-availability limitation in the historical Phase 5A audit. It does not verify a later organizer amendment or the separately delivered briefing case.

## Exact rubric weights

| Official aspect | Initial selection, p. 6, G1 | Final, p. 7, G2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fungsionalitas | 35% | 25% |
| Kesesuaian dengan Studi Kasus | 25% | 20% |
| UI/UX | 20% | 15% |
| Kualitas Kode & Pemeliharaan | 10% | 10% |
| Dokumentasi | 10% | 10% |
| Presentasi dan Q&A | — | 20% |
| Total | 100% | 100% |

Page 6 indicators: feature completeness/function against purpose; fit to theme/case and its problem; interface quality, navigation, flow clarity, responsiveness and experience; code organization, structure, components, understandability, maintenance and extensibility; documentation completeness and clarity. Page 7 presentation/Q&A indicators: clear explanation of product/features/purpose, implementation and decisions; mastery of product/technology; ability to answer and defend development decisions. Initial scores select five finalists; final judging is independent and determines the final outcome (pp. 6–8). These verified weights do not turn the historical Phase 5A diagnostic into an official judge score.
