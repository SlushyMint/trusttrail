# Email realism and art revision — working direction

29 October 2026. Review-prototype revision requested by the Product Owner. The existing production application and canonical Chapter 1 content/events remain unchanged.

## Research translated into design

Primary references: [Gmail product presentation](https://workspace.google.com/products/gmail/), [Google's toolbar guide](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/2473038?hl=en), [desktop sender-detail disclosure](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/180707?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en), and [conversation/inbox structure](https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/9259955?hl=en). Public reference images were inspected in the ignored research cache, not shipped as game UI or used as generated-art inputs.

The goal is the familiar grammar of reading a Gmail message: pale application chrome around a white reading surface, a restrained toolbar, subject before sender, a circular sender avatar, compact timestamp, “kepada saya” details disclosure, ordinary paragraph spacing and a blue underlined candidate address. The fictional client is named **Mail**, with Ari's fictional local account. No Google account, authentication indicator or real mail connection is implied.

The previous amber controls interrupted the letter and made it look like an educational worksheet. The revision separates the in-world message from Ari's inspection tools. Address inspection is a deliberate sender-details disclosure; deadline inspection is an explicitly labeled examination action; destination inspection is available beside the inert displayed link. Opening a message or focusing a control still awards nothing. Important evidence is not automatically revealed, and the three existing inspection events retain their original meanings.

A narrow mail navigation area groups the two existing fictional messages. The independent directory remains a separate investigation source. Any visible control must perform a useful local action; no fake search, compose, archive or delete button is added merely to fill a toolbar. Email text stays complete, readable and in HTML. On phones, one reading pane replaces the desktop rail.

## Art revision criteria

“Not AI slop” is a visual quality requirement, not a claim that generated art is handmade. Retain transparent provenance. Replace generic cinematic rendering with a specific graphic-novel treatment: deliberate ink contours, broad flat color masses, limited two-step shadows, restrained texture and no ambient halos outside character silhouettes.

Ari retains the original identity: warm brown skin, short wavy black hair/forelock, teal overshirt and ivory T-shirt. Keep face proportions, sleeve/pocket construction and lighting coherent across three expressions. A thoughtful pose must have plausible hands and shoulder connections. Expressions show uncertainty/readiness without signaling a correct choice.

The room should reflect an ordinary Indonesian graduate's job search: a practical desk, unmarked portfolio, simple fan, louvered window and neighboring tiled roofs. Use fewer purposeful objects, clear furniture perspective and a usable quiet foreground for Ari. Avoid decorative plant collections, arbitrary picture clusters, excessive amber glow, fabricated writing and uniform grunge over everything. All clues remain outside the artwork.

Review each chosen asset at full size and at its actual in-game scale. Check hand anatomy, feature consistency, clean alpha edges, desk/window perspective, color hierarchy and whether an object earns its place in Ari's story. Keep previous variants for comparison. Judge the assembled screen as well as isolated illustrations; do not use a self-assigned score as proof of quality.
