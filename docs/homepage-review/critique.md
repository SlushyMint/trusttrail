# Visual review

Direction: **The page is the object**, seed **22990416**. Ari's digital recruitment invitation becomes the entry portal into TrustTrail OS. The live element is a separate sender-address inspection.

Reviewed the final desktop, tablet and phone screenshots, including every screenshot from `.taste-skill/homepage-final-reviewed/`, and the full-size 360px captures in this folder.

## Look-and-fix rounds

First round: retained authentic game colors and type, replaced the OS welcome window with a proper entry page, and used actual Chapter 1 captures instead of invented product chrome.

Second round: enlarged the real app crops; increased running text to 16px; made all visible controls at least 44px tall; removed small kicker labels; replaced repeated icon cards with chapter rows; placed a short actionable explanation beside the revealed address so mobile users receive useful feedback immediately.

Final round: no overlap, missing image, clipped heading, horizontal overflow, empty reveal area or inconsistent frame found. Final skill inspector: 0 failures and one defended no-video warning, with the two recorded allowances in `design-notes.md`.
