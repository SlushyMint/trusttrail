# TrustTrail homepage redesign

The homepage is an entry into Ari's investigation. Its primary evidence is the real Chapter 1 interface, captured from a clean, isolated browser context before any clue was inspected. The navy, steel, teal, square window chrome, Silkscreen headings, Inter reading text, signal mark and pixel icons all come from production Lacak OS. No dependencies were added.

## Argument and copy direction

Audience: Indonesian students, internship seekers, and MIT-WEEK judges. First-screen action: start the actual investigation, or resume an existing run.

1. This is an interactive cybersecurity investigation game: the recruitment mystery, real OS screenshot, and existing start/resume action.
2. Digital deception has real consequences: three source-attributed Indonesian statistics with individual definitions and historical reporting periods.
3. Investigation means checking evidence: a single sender-address inspection with immediate feedback and a separate live notebook.
4. Ari's story continues across three chapters: Chapter 1 is available; Chapters 2–3 are explicitly upcoming.
5. This is a safe fictional exercise: no real passwords, OTPs, identity documents or account registration; progress remains local.

Subject inventory: Ari; an unsolicited recruitment invitation; NovaTech; the empty evidence journal; the independently found company directory; Lacak OS; Saku on phones; coastal relay wallpaper; stepped signal mark; teal inspection controls; Silkscreen; local event history.

Idea: On this page, Ari's recruitment invitation becomes the portal into TrustTrail OS. The live interaction lets visitors inspect a different fictional invitation without seeing Chapter 1's clue answers.

## Design skill application

Applied `design-taste-frontend`, `web-design-guidelines`, React performance guidance, and current React and Playwright documentation through Context7.

Dealt direction: **The page is the object**, seed **22990416**, recorded in `.taste-skill/homepage/direction.json`.

Brand and content adaptations: the invitation is a digital mail window rather than a printed wedding card; the requested real OS preview determines the landscape proportions and split hero. The three unrelated statistics cannot form the dealt additive receipt. Each gets its own value, definition, period and source disclosure. The one email interaction replaces a six-to-nine-moment carousel; there is only one clue. Three chapter rows replace six-to-nine accordion items, since only three chapters exist. The final invitation becomes the safety/start panel. No invented content was added to satisfy an incompatible slot.

Motion is confined to immediate control feedback with a reduced-motion fallback. A continuous moving image, footer curtain and eight decorative entrance animations would conflict with the approved game's still presentation and the brief's emphasis on immediate entry and purposeful motion. The inspector's `still-page` exemption is recorded rather than adding motion just to meet a count. No photographs, footage, stock portraits or externally hosted imagery are used.

The inspector also misclassified the required “Baca siaran pers OJK” citation inside a closed native disclosure as an eyebrow above the following section. There are no visual kicker labels on the final page; this source-link false positive is the only `eyebrow` allowance. The final skill run uses its supported 1440×900, 768×1024 and 390×844 viewports. Its default height for 360px is only 225px; that generates an irrelevant long-interface warning. The actual 360×844 layout is independently measured, photographed and browser-tested in `browser-results.json`.

## State boundaries

`OperatingSystem` remains mounted while the homepage is displayed; its shell is hidden with the native `hidden` attribute. The gameplay provider, reducer, content, scoring, persistence and game route remain intact. The homepage reuses `StartGameButton`, `SaveNotice`, `ProgressControls`, and production `PixelIcon`.

The email exercise owns one local boolean and imports no game actions or storage functions. Opening or closing it cannot dispatch game events. Its `.example` address is inert text. Its feedback explains that a name or different domain cannot establish legitimacy on its own. Returning home preserves in-memory OS state; reloading restores canonical saved progress as before.

No duration claim is displayed: 12–18 minutes in GDD.md is a design target awaiting validation. Chapters 2–3 use spoiler-free editorial teasers derived from the GDD and are explicitly unavailable, not interactive links.
