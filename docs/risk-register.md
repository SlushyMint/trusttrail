## Residual risk policy

No system can infer real-world offer safety from gameplay. Local saves are editable and clearable, ordinary host logs exist, multi-tab simultaneous writes cannot be guaranteed atomic, and mobile emulation cannot establish every device's behavior. State those practical limits. Accept only documented nonblocking residuals; no sensitive-input, game-completion, public-access or core-accessibility blocker may be hidden by a disclaimer. Reassess after the C1 slice and before release.
